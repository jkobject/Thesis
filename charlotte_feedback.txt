Chapter 0 still needs substantial revision. Beyond being too broad, it is not written in a consistently scientific register: parts of the language are overly informal, rhetorical, or imprecise for a PhD thesis. The structure is also problematic. The chapter opens with “The promises of cellular biology” and then moves into short sections on “Drug Design” and “Other Applications,” which gives the beginning more the character of a broad motivational essay than of a rigorous thesis introduction. This is then followed by a long sequence of fairly elementary background material on RNA, gene expression, sequencing, and general AI concepts before the text reaches the actual problem setting and scientific aims much later. As a result, the chapter does not yet provide the focused, technically grounded entry point that the rest of the thesis needs. I still suggest removing "Personal Motivation” and providing a complete rewrite of Chapter 0 with a proper 1. Introduction and then 2. Background, as already explicitly mentioned in my previous feedback.
